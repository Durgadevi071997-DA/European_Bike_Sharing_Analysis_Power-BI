Introduction

The European Bike Sharing Analysis project focuses on understanding the usage patterns, customer behavior, operational efficiency, and environmental impact of bike sharing systems across major European cities.

Bike sharing services have become one of the most popular urban transportation solutions in Europe.

Cities like London, Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin, Madrid, and Copenhagen actively promote sustainable transportation.

The increasing traffic congestion and environmental pollution have encouraged governments to support bike sharing programs.

This analysis helps organizations make data-driven decisions for improving bike availability, customer satisfaction, and operational planning.

The project uses historical bike trip data collected from different European bike sharing platforms.

User type

Bike ID
